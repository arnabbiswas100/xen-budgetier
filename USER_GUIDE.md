# Budgetier — Quick Cheatsheet & User Guide

Welcome to **Budgetier**! Here is everything you need to know to take full control of your monthly spending.

---

## 1. Setting Your Monthly Budget
1. On the **Home** (Dashboard) screen, tap your monthly budget amount (or tap the **Set Budget** button).
2. Enter your total allowance for the month (e.g., `10000`).
3. Tap **Save**.
*Note: Your budget automatically carries forward into next month!*

---

## 2. Daily Recommended Spending (The Magic Metric)
- **"Daily Rec"** tells you the exact maximum amount you can spend today without exceeding your monthly budget.
- It dynamically recalculates based on how much money is remaining and how many days are left in the month.
- When you spend less than your daily rec, your allowance for future days increases!

---

## 3. Logging Expenses
- Tap the **+ Add Expense** button at the bottom of any screen.
- Enter the amount, pick a category, and optionally select a sub-category or note.
- **Accidentally deleted an expense?** A notification will pop up with an **[Undo]** button for 5 seconds to instantly restore it.

---

## 4. Recurring Expenses (Subscriptions & Rent)
- Check the **"Recurring monthly expense"** box when adding rent, gym memberships, or subscriptions.
- At the start of a new month, tap **"Apply Recurring"** to log them in one tap.
- To cancel or stop a recurring bill, go to **Settings** → **Recurring Expenses** and tap **Stop**.

---

## 5. Category Budgets & Warnings
- Tap **Category Budgets** to set limits on categories like *Food*, *Transport*, or *Shopping*.
- Progress bars show you how much you've spent:
  - **Green**: Under 80%
  - **Yellow**: 80%–100% of category limit
  - **Red**: Overspent!

---

## 6. Full Data Backups & CSV Export
Go to **Settings**:
- **Export JSON (Full Backup)**: Downloads a complete copy of your financial history. Save it to Google Drive or WhatsApp to restore anytime.
- **Import JSON**: Restores your data if you switch to a new phone.
- **Export Month (CSV)**: Generates a spreadsheet file compatible with Excel and Google Sheets.
