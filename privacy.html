1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Budgetier mobile application ("Budgetier", "the App", "we", "our").

Budgetier was designed with strict privacy-by-design principles. The application functions entirely offline on your mobile device as an offline utility tool. We have intentionally engineered Budgetier so that we cannot see, access, collect, or monetize any of your personal financial records.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because Budgetier operates as a standalone client-side application, we do not collect any personal data:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or government IDs.
  • No Financial Credentials: Budgetier never connects to your bank accounts, credit cards, or UPI apps. You enter your expense numbers manually.
  • No Device or Location Data: We do not read your GPS coordinates, contacts, media library, or device IMEI.
  • No Analytics or Crash Logs: We do not embed telemetry libraries, session trackers, or third-party monitoring SDKs.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data entered into Budgetier (such as your monthly budget, transaction records, custom categories, and theme settings) is stored strictly on your local device using standard on-device storage APIs (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).

Important: Because your data exists only on your device, we maintain no central server backups of your entries. If you lose your phone or clear the app's cache without creating a backup, your records cannot be recovered by us.

4. Data Backup, Export & Portability

Budgetier gives you full, unrestricted ownership of your data at all times:

  • Export: You can export all your financial records to a human-readable JSON file directly to your local file system or cloud drive anytime via the Settings menu.
  • Import / Restore: You can restore previously exported JSON backup files on any device with one tap.
  • Deletion: You can purge all stored records instantly using the "Clear All Data" button in Settings, or by simply uninstalling the application from your Android device.

5. Network Usage & Permissions

Budgetier requires no invasive device permissions (such as Contacts, Camera, SMS, Microphone, or Location).

The application runs without an active internet connection. Any network capabilities are strictly limited to standard native webview container routines and local rendering.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Budgetier contains zero third-party advertisements and integrates no third-party marketing services or ad networks. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties under any circumstances.

7. Children's Privacy

Budgetier does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Since the application collects no personal information whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect future feature enhancements or legal requirements. When updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly. Because we do not collect contact information, we encourage users to review this page periodically.
