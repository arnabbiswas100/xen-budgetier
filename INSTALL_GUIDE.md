# How to Install Budgetier on Your Android Phone

Thank you for purchasing **Budgetier**! 

Budgetier is a 100% offline, privacy-first personal finance app. It does not track you, does not show ads, and does not require any account or internet connection.

Follow these 3 simple steps to install it on your Android phone.

---

## Step 1: Download the APK
1. Open the Google Drive download link you received on your Android phone.
2. Tap on **`Budgetier.apk`** and tap **Download**.

---

## Step 2: Allow Installation
Because you are installing directly from Google Drive rather than Google Play, Android will ask for permission:
1. When the download completes, tap the file in your notifications or Downloads folder.
2. If Android displays: *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings** on the prompt.
   - Toggle **Allow from this source** (or "Install unknown apps") to **ON**.
   - Tap the back button to return to the installer.

---

## Step 3: Tap Install
1. Tap **Install**.
2. Tap **Open** when installation finishes.
3. The **Budgetier** icon is now on your home screen!

---

## Offline & Privacy Guarantee
- **Airplane Mode Test**: Turn on Airplane mode and open Budgetier. Everything works instantly.
- **Your Data**: All your budgets, categories, and expenses are saved directly on your phone in local storage. Nothing ever leaves your device.
- **Backups**: Go to the **Settings** tab anytime and tap **Export JSON (Full Backup)** to save a backup to your Downloads folder or Google Drive.
